Before setting a process

  1. Confirm exact grade, standard and material condition.
  2. Check section size, geometry and required properties.
  3. Confirm furnace uniformity and actual part temperature.
  4. Specify quench medium, agitation and distortion controls.
  5. Follow customer specification and approved procedure.

Hardness conversion reference

HRC, HB and HV are not linearly interchangeable. Use a recognized conversion table for the material group and hardness range. Refer to ASTM E140 or ISO 18265; no unsupported conversion is generated here.

Process fundamentals

Normalizing

Heat into the grade-specific austenitizing range, hold as required, then still-air cool.

Quenching

Rapid cooling from the austenitic condition. Cooling rate and hardenability govern transformation.

Tempering

Reheat hardened steel below its lower critical temperature to adjust properties.

Carburizing

Diffuse carbon into a suitable low-carbon steel surface before hardening.

Metallurgical inspection checklist

  • Record sample location: surface, mid-radius or core.
  • Record magnification, etchant and preparation method.
  • Assess ferrite/pearlite distribution, grain size and banding.
  • Correlate microstructure with hardness and process history.
  • Compare against the applicable customer/product specification.
A micrograph alone may not prove compliance. Consider chemistry, hardness, grain size and process records.
